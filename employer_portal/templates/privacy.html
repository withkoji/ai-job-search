APPLICANT PRIVACY

Your hiring information

What this portal collects

When you apply, we save your name, email address, optional resume or portfolio link, introduction, and consent timestamp. The hiring business can add review notes, a hiring status and interview details.

Who can access it

The business receiving your application can review it in its private workspace. The portal operator can access stored records to operate the service and handle support or deletion requests. Candidate records and hiring notes are not displayed on the public job board.

How it is used

Your information supports application review and interview coordination. Submission does not guarantee an interview or offer. This portal does not perform background checks, sell applicant records, or automatically send email or calendar invitations. External resume links remain subject to the service where you host them.

Corrections, withdrawal and deletion

{% if contact %}
